6. What the Service does and does not do

6.1 The Service does:

  • Answer calls in the languages set out in Schedule A (English and Spanish by default), when the Firm's rules say to: line busy, after a set number of rings, no answer, evenings, weekends or holidays.
  • Introduce itself as the Firm's answering service and tell Callers they are speaking with an automated assistant.
  • Tell every Caller at the start of the call that the call may be recorded.
  • Take an Intake using the Firm's script and answer routine questions the Firm has approved.
  • Flag urgent calls at the top of each morning Digest, under the Firm's escalation rules, and send alerts by email, and by text if text alerts are chosen in Schedule A and set up for the Firm before go-live.
  • Send the Digest each morning at the time and to the email address set out in Schedule A.

6.2 The Service does not:

  • Give legal advice, assess a case, quote fees or promise representation.
  • Create a lawyer–client relationship between the Firm and any Caller. Only the Firm decides whom it represents.
  • Transfer live calls to the Firm. The Service takes a detailed message instead.
  • Book consultations into a calendar. This is not available yet; No Hang Up will confirm timing in writing if and when it is.
  • Act as an emergency line. A Caller who says they are in danger is told to hang up and call 911.

6.3 AI-generated output. The Service uses artificial intelligence to hold conversations, transcribe calls and write Intakes and Digests. Calls are not monitored live by a person. AI can mishear, misunderstand or summarize inaccurately. Recordings and transcripts are kept for 30 days so the Firm can check what was said. The Firm should confirm important details directly with the Caller before relying on them.
