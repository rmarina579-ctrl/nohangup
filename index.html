07 — Questions

What lawyers ask first.

When does the AI answer?

Whenever you want her to. A common setup is overflow + after-hours: she picks up when your line is busy, after a set number of rings, or when nobody answers — plus evenings, weekends, and holidays. You define the rules; she follows them.

What happens to my current receptionist?

She stays. This isn't a replacement — it's her backup. Sick days, vacations, lunch hour: those calls get answered instead of dying in voicemail. And her mornings get easier, because calls arrive triaged instead of as a pile of sticky notes.

What does the caller experience?

A natural phone conversation. She introduces herself as your firm's answering service, takes a structured intake, and handles routine questions. She flags urgent calls at the top of your morning Digest, per your rules, and sends alerts by email — and by text if you choose text alerts in your setup form (Schedule A) and we set them up before go-live.

What do I get each morning?

A triage digest: urgent at the top, new intakes with full context, routine at the bottom. Thirty seconds to know who to call first.

What if she gets something wrong?

She works from your intake script — same questions, same way, every time. Every call she answers is logged by our voice platform, Retell AI, and appears in your Digest, so you see exactly what was captured.

Is my clients' information safe?

Intake data goes only to you and to the providers listed in our Privacy Policy (voice AI, email, payments). It's never sold, and we never use identifiable information to train AI. Our voice provider, Retell AI, may use de-identified data to improve its models — details in our Privacy Policy. We're happy to walk through our data handling on a call before you start.

Does she speak Spanish?

Yes — English and Spanish out of the box. Ask us about other languages.

How does the $97 pilot work?

Seven days, up to 150 minutes, live on your lines — counted from the day your line goes live, after setup. If she's not earning her keep, email us before the end of day 7 and you get the $97 back — no forms, no hard feelings. If you stay, $400 (plus HST when applicable) is charged automatically on day 8 (your first month's balance), then $497/month (plus HST when applicable).

What does $497/month include?

500 minutes (about 150 calls), $0.60 per extra minute, no setup fee for founding firms, month to month.

Can she book consultations into my calendar?

Not yet. It's on our roadmap, and we'll tell you in writing when it's available.

What if the service goes down?

We monitor the service and work to restore it fast. Where your phone setup allows it, calls fall back to your normal voicemail during an outage — exactly where you are today. If an outage lasts more than an hour, we tell you and credit the affected days on your next bill.
