05 — Questions

What lawyers ask first.

When does the AI answer?

Whenever you want her to. Most firms set her as overflow + after-hours: she picks up when your line is busy, after a set number of rings, or when nobody answers — plus evenings, weekends, and holidays. You define the rules; she follows them.

What happens to my current receptionist?

She stays. This isn't a replacement — it's her backup. Sick days, vacations, lunch hour: those calls get answered instead of dying in voicemail. And her mornings get easier, because every call arrives triaged instead of as a pile of sticky notes.

What does the caller experience?

A natural phone conversation. She introduces herself as your firm's answering service, takes a structured intake, and handles routine questions. If the caller needs a lawyer urgently, she flags it at the top of your morning digest, per your rules. (Live patch-through and instant alerts are on the roadmap — we'll confirm timing during your pilot setup.)

What do I get each morning?

A triage digest: urgent at the top, new intakes with full context, routine at the bottom. Thirty seconds to know who to call first.

What if she gets something wrong?

She works from your intake script — same questions, same way, every time. Every call is logged and lands in your digest, so you see exactly what was captured.

Is my clients' information safe?

Intake data goes only to you — never shared, never sold. A full privacy policy comes with your pilot agreement.

Does she speak Spanish?

Yes — English and Spanish out of the box, more languages available.

How does the $97 pilot work?

Seven days, up to 200 minutes, live on your lines. If she's not earning her keep by day 7, you get the $97 back — no forms, no hard feelings. If you stay, $400 is charged automatically on day 8 (your first month's balance), then $497/month.

What does $497/month include?

800 minutes (~250 calls), $0.60/min overage, no setup fee for founding firms, month to month.

Can she book consultations into my calendar?

On the roadmap — we'll confirm timing during your pilot setup.

What if the service goes down?

We monitor the service and work to restore it fast. Where your phone setup allows it, calls fall back to your normal voicemail during an outage — exactly where you are today.
