What happens next

  • Checking the box below and paying is how you accept the Terms and Conditions. No separate signature is needed.
  • Right after payment, you go straight to a short setup form (about 10 minutes) for your firm's details, intake questions and escalation rules.
  • When you submit it, we email you a welcome note with a copy of the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy for your records.
  • Within 5 business days, we build your receptionist and you test her on a private line. Nothing touches your clients until you approve.
  • Once you approve, you forward your office number to your new No Hang Up line. It takes about 5 minutes, and we send simple step-by-step instructions with your test line. Your 7-day pilot begins the day forwarding is on. You're only charged again on day 8, and only if you stay.

Good to know

  • We don't guarantee that every call will be answered or that every intake will be complete or accurate. Calls answered by the Service and logged by Retell AI appear in your Digest so you can check (Terms §9.2).

  • She's an AI. She can mishear or summarize inaccurately, so confirm important details with the caller before relying on them.
  • She doesn't give legal advice, doesn't transfer live calls, and isn't an emergency line.
  • Intake data is never sold, and No Hang Up never uses identifiable information to train AI models. Retell AI may use de-identified, aggregated call data to develop, train, and improve its AI models. See Privacy Policy §4–§5 and Retell AI's Terms.
