Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026. Download this version (PDF).

In short: No Hang Up answers phone calls for law firms. We collect what a caller tells us so the firm can call them back, we send it only to that firm and the providers listed below, and we never sell it or use identifiable information to train AI models. In Canada, this information is protected by the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act.

1. Who we are and our role

No Hang Up operates in Toronto, Ontario (nohangup.ca). We provide an AI answering service to law firms.

When you call a law firm and we answer, we act on that firm's behalf. The firm decides what we ask and what happens with your information. We handle it only to pass it to the firm, under our Terms and Conditions, which each firm accepts electronically before the service starts.

2. What we collect, and why

We collect only what the firm needs to call you back and understand your situation.

What Why
Your name and phone number So the firm can return your call
What happened, your injuries, the other party involved So the firm can understand your matter before calling
The time and length of your call For the firm's records and our billing
A recording and transcript of the call So the firm can check what was said

For law firms that are our customers, we also keep contact and billing details. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers.

3. Your call

The assistant tells you at the start of the call that it is an automated answering service and that the call may be recorded. You can choose not to answer any question, or ask to leave only your name and number. If you are in danger, hang up and call 911; this is not an emergency line.

4. How we use AI

  • The assistant you speak with is an artificial intelligence system. What you say is processed by our voice provider to understand you, reply, and write a transcript and summary for the firm.
  • Calls are not monitored live by a person. The firm receives the summary and can check the recording.
  • AI can make mistakes, so the firm confirms important details with you directly.
  • The assistant does not give legal advice and cannot accept you as a client. Only the law firm can do that.
  • We never use identifiable information to train AI models. Our voice provider, Retell AI, states in its own terms that, unless recording is opted out, it may use de-identified and aggregated call data to develop, train, and improve its AI models. We do not opt out of recording because recordings and transcripts are needed to provide the Service. We keep only aggregated statistics, such as the number and length of calls, that cannot identify you.

5. Who we share it with

Your information goes to the law firm you called, and to the providers listed below, who handle it to provide the Service, under contracts that limit them to that purpose, except as described below (Retell AI's de-identified training use, telecom carriers acting as independent controllers, and each provider's own terms). We do not sell it, rent it or use it for advertising. Retell AI itself uses subprocessors to carry calls — telecom carriers (who may process data as independent controllers for billing/fraud), AI model providers, and cloud hosting (AWS) — listed at trust.retellai.com/subprocessors. Retell AI may add subprocessors under its Data Processing Addendum with notice. No Hang Up has signed Retell AI's Data Processing Addendum (GDPR/CCPA, not PIPEDA). Please do not send payment-card numbers, Social Insurance Numbers (SIN), passwords, or biometric data. Intake may include health-related information you voluntarily provide for legal intake, such as injuries, hospital or doctor visits, and treatment including physical therapy. That information is sent only to the law firm you called, for legal intake, and is handled as personal information under PIPEDA as described in this Policy. This is not a healthcare service. For Retell AI's own terms, see retellai.com/legal/terms-of-service (October 7, 2026) and retellai.com/legal/privacy-policy (July 20, 2026). If Retell AI materially changes its terms in a way that conflicts with this Policy, we will notify our customer firms promptly.

Provider What they do
Retell AI Carries the call, runs the voice assistant, and records and transcribes the call
Google (Google Workspace) Delivers the morning summary and alerts to the firm by email
Stripe Processes pilot and subscription payments; we never see or store full card numbers

We disclose information to anyone else only if the law requires it. Where the law allows, we tell the firm first so it can respond.

6. Where it is stored

Primarily United States. Our voice provider stores call data primarily in the United States, where it may be accessed by courts and authorities under US law. Retell AI and its telecom providers may process data in other jurisdictions as needed to carry calls.

7. How long we keep it

Information Kept for
Call recordings and transcripts (Retell AI retains some basic metadata after deletion) 30 days
Intake details and morning summaries 90 days
A firm's data after it cancels or the Terms and Conditions end (except data Retell AI must retain as required by law or per standard backup retention) Deleted within 30 days

After that, we delete it. The law firm keeps its own copy of what we sent and is responsible for it from then on. Billing records for our customer firms are kept as long as tax law requires.

8. How we protect it

Only authorized No Hang Up staff and the providers needed to run the Service (Retell AI and its subprocessors, Google, Stripe) can access the data, only as needed to run the Service. Per Retell AI's Data Processing Addendum, data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We enable two-step sign-in on accounts that hold data where the provider offers it.

If we learn that your information may have been accessed without permission, we tell the firm right away and, where the law requires, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and you.

9. Our website and payment pages

We do not use advertising pixels or tracking for advertising, and we do not sell or share data with advertisers. Payment pages are run by Stripe, which may use its own cookies to process payments and prevent fraud, under Stripe's privacy policy.

10. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Because we act for the law firm you called, we may pass your request to that firm. We answer within 30 days where possible, and within 45 days where we must obtain data from our providers.

11. Contact and complaints

No Hang Up, support@nohangup.ca, Toronto, Ontario.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any change to this policy on nohangup.ca with its new date, and tell our customer firms by email before it takes effect.
