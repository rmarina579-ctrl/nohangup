Privacy Policy

In short: No Hang Up answers phone calls for law firms. We collect what a caller tells us so the firm can call them back, we send it only to that firm and the providers listed below, and we never sell it or use identifiable information to train AI models. In Canada, this information is protected by the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act.

Who we are and our role

No Hang Up is operated by Marina Mitchell in Toronto, Ontario (nohangup.ca). We provide an AI answering service to law firms.

When you call a law firm and we answer, we act on that firm's behalf. The firm decides what we ask and what happens with your information. We handle it only to pass it to the firm, under a written agreement with them.

What we collect, and why

We collect only what the firm needs to call you back and understand your situation.

WhatWhy
Your name and phone numberSo the firm can return your call
What happened, your injuries, the other party involvedSo the firm can understand your matter before calling
The time and length of your callFor the firm's records and our billing
A recording and transcript of the callSo the firm can check what was said

The assistant tells you at the start of the call that it is an automated answering service and that the call may be recorded. You can choose not to answer any question, or ask to leave only your name and number.

For law firms that are our customers, we also keep contact and billing details. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers.

Who we share it with

Your information goes to the law firm you called, and to the providers listed below who handle it only on our behalf — nowhere else. We do not sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising, and we never use identifiable information to train AI models.

To run the service, a small number of providers handle the data on our behalf, under contracts that limit them to that purpose:

Where it is stored: United States. Our voice provider stores call data in the United States, where it may be accessed by courts and authorities under US law.

We disclose information to anyone else only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it and how we protect it

After that, we delete it. The law firm keeps its own copy of what we sent and is responsible for it from then on.

Only Marina Mitchell can access the data. We use providers that encrypt data in storage and in transit, and we turn on two-step sign-in on every account that holds it. If we learn that your information may have been accessed without permission, we tell the firm right away and, where the law requires, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and you.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Because we act for the law firm you called, we may pass your request to that firm; we answer within 30 days.

Contact: Marina Mitchell, No Hang Up, marina@nohangup.ca, Toronto, Ontario.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

We will post any change to this policy on nohangup.ca with its new date, and tell our customer firms by email before it takes effect.
